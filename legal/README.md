# Legal templates

**These are templates, not legal advice.** Nothing in them confirms that a clause is valid for your company, country, or customers. Have a qualified lawyer in your country review and adapt all three before you sign anyone up.

Fill every `[BRACKET]`. Where a bracket asks you to state a fact about a third party (the AI provider's retention and training terms, your hosting provider and region), read their current published terms and write what they actually say.

If you operate from Morocco, ask your lawyer about Law 09-08 on personal data protection and any declaration or authorization needed from the CNDP, and about transfers of data abroad (invoices go to an AI provider and to your hosting provider). Also check rules for customers located in other countries.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.template.md` | The agreement with each customer |
| `PRIVACY_POLICY.template.md` | What you collect and do with it |
| `DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT.template.md` | For business customers whose invoices contain personal data |

The data descriptions match what the software actually does (see `SECURITY.md` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`). If you change the software, update the documents.
